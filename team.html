How we work

Six principles.

  • Value creation first. Every recommendation is measured against whether it creates value or quietly consumes it.
  • Quantitative wherever pragmatic. If a question can be answered with a number, we answer it with a number.
  • Transparent modelling. Assumptions are tabled with the basis for each one. Nothing important is buried in a cell.
  • Thorough diligence. We would rather find the problem than be the ones who missed it.
  • Clear messaging. A board should be able to see the hinge points of a decision on one page.
  • Collaborative independence. We form our own view first, then discuss where it aligns with yours and where it does not. The disagreements are usually the valuable part.
